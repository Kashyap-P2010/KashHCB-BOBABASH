History of za boba!

bubble tea now known as za boba coz its kinda cute name now and genz style ig, was originated in Taiwan during 1908s🤓

  • 1949 - haand shaken tea: hand shaken tea brewed using a martini mixer to create a rich sweety foammy 🫧 layer
  • 1986-1988 PEARLS(LWK BEST PART OF ZA INVENTION): Tu Zonghe of Hanlin Tea Room in Tainan (not taiwan hehe) experimented w/addin fen yuan(small tapioca ballz) to green tea
  • 1988 - Lin Hsiu Hui, a product development manager at Chun Shui Tang in Taichung, playfully dropped sweeetened tapicoa pudding into her iced miilk tea during a meeting
  • BOTH TEA HOUSES HAD ONE FULL DECADE LONG LEGAL BATTLE over the invention, which court dismissed(lol) in 2019, as its considered common culture
